Allele Mono is a monospace typeface for reading DNA, RNA and protein sequences, and code. Letters that are easily confused at small sizes, such as C and G, H and M, b and d, or i and dotless i, are drawn to stay distinguishable in dense sequence alignments and source code. It fully supports Turkish.

Allele Mono is designed by Ozge A. Cavus, AIstanbul Research Group. To contribute, see github.com/aistanbulresearch/allele-mono.
